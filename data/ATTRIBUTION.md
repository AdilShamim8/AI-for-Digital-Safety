# Dataset attribution and versions

Source organization: HerWILL. Event: AI for Digital Safety Datathon 2026.
The user supplied the datasets through the shared Drive folder
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1TNU6bITzInZjTCTT3RrhAKFp4eIyX_VQ
and described the competition as private.

The accompanying `docs/competition_details.docx` lists
Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/
This is the license statement found in the supplied document; the original
competition license page and full permission records were not independently
retrieved. The repository's MIT license does not replace this statement or
grant rights to third-party material.

`herwill_2026/train.csv`, `test.csv`, and `sample_submission.csv` are verbatim
bytes from the supplied 2026 ZIP. `submission.csv` is the separately supplied
prediction file. The original ZIP is retained in `archives/`.

`four_class_version/` preserves the separately supplied loose CSVs unchanged.
Their extra rows and label 3 are outside the three-class competition
description. Their source and independent redistribution terms are not
established by that description; do not present them as the official 2026
release. The separate 2024 ZIP is retained as an original archive and is not
used for the three-class paper's statistics.

No raw file was deduplicated, relabeled, merged, or otherwise modified.
Documented statistics and checks are separate derived analysis files.
