# ICIEV 2026 submission steps

Official rules checked 11 October 2026:
https://cennser.org/ICIEV/paper-submission.html
https://cennser.org/ICIEV/important-dates.html

1. Resolve the evidence and author blockers in `REVISION_CHECKLIST.md`.
   The present package is a complete buildable revision with explicit limits,
   with a completed new LR comparison; it is not a historical fitted-state replay or a verified competition submission.
2. Use the **IJCVSP, two-column, regular-paper** route. The conference specifies
   6–10 pages; this PDF is 10 pages including references. The IJABC route uses
   a different one-column template and 10–20 pages. Choose a CMT option by its
   actual label; no unobserved menu choices have been inferred.
3. Sign in or register at
   https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/ICIEV2026/Submission/Index
4. If a paper record already exists, edit that record when allowed. Otherwise
   create a new paper submission. Avoid creating a duplicate for the same paper.
5. Enter the exact title and current abstract. Enter **all three authors** in
   the approved order, starting with Shamim Hasan, and accurate email addresses.
   ICIEV states authors cannot be added or changed after initial submission.
   If the existing record is wrong and editing is locked, request correction
   from the organizers. No author details have been invented in this package.
6. Select the appropriate topic/track from the options actually displayed.
   Verify the regular IJCVSP route rather than assuming the former ACML/LNCS
   template applies.
7. Choose named-case A (`paper/iciev/main.pdf`) or public-datathon B (`paper/iciev/main_anonymized.pdf`), then upload that PDF. The initial instructions request PDF,
   not the `.tex` file or repository ZIP. Inspect the uploaded preview and page
   count before saving/submitting.
8. Keep the PDF anonymous: no author names, affiliations or acknowledgements.
   Do not activate `author-details.tex`. Do not link the public named GitHub
   repository from the blind manuscript or upload its identifying archives as
   anonymous supplements. Use a properly anonymized supplement if the venue
   permits and requests supporting evidence.
9. Retain the paper ID and submission confirmation. A correct local PDF/CSV
   or a save message does not establish successful portal submission.
10. The official regular deadline is **20 October 2026**. Closing time/timezone
    was not established; verify in CMT or with the organizers. After acceptance,
    follow camera-ready instructions, add confirmed author metadata, and
    complete applicable disclosures/forms. The dates page lists camera-ready
    submission on 15 November 2026.

The repository PR and ZIP are the requested handoff. Creating a PR does not
submit the paper to ICIEV, and this work does not merge that PR.
